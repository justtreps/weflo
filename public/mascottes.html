The mascots

Twelve states of the same duck. Each one has a job: it carries the product's voice in the moments where the interface speaks to the merchant — waiting, success, error, empty.

The cast · 12 states

01 Happy

Arms wide, glowing halo.

Used for Store published. The only celebration screen in the flow.
02 Surprised

Wings clapped over the beak.

Used for The AI proposes an unexpected result — a theme, a suggested price.
03 Jaded

Half-closed eyes.

Used for A catalogue that's been empty for a while, or a draft never published.
04 Asleep

Eyes shut, Zzz.

Used for Store paused, account inactive, session expired.
05 Curious

Big round eyes.

Used for First screen. The creation prompt, while everything is still open.
06 Worried

Drooping brows.

Used for Gentle warning: low stock, incomplete product page.
07 Idea

Wing to beak, lightbulb.

Used for A copilot suggestion. This is the face of the recommendation.
08 Devil

Horns, smirk.

Used for Aggressive mode: full rebuild, slashed prices, offensive campaign.
09 Anxious

Blue jitters.

Used for Confirming an irreversible action — delete, unpublish.
10 In love

Heart eyes, music notes.

Used for First sale, revenue milestone crossed.
11 Angry

Furrowed brows, steam.

Used for Technical failure: import failed, payment declined, generation crashed.
12 Crying

Blue tears.

Used for 404, abandoned cart, end of free trial.

Usage rules

One duck on screen at a time

The mascot marks a moment, it doesn't decorate. Two ducks visible at once and the message dilutes.

Never on a dark background for 01

Its halo was drawn for a light background: on dark it reads as a pale ring. Ask for a halo-free version if you need one.

Yellow belongs to the mascot

Beak orange carries the interface actions. If yellow also becomes a button colour, the duck stops standing out.

No duck on the money screens

Payment, billing, sales data: these screens need calm. The mascot would cost them credibility.
